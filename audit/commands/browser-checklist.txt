How a wake works
Point and pinch. Quest Space Setup (or the emulator room) already holds furniture-sized boxes. A hand ray draws a dotted outline and a label chip. Pinch, or click in the browser.
A face blooms. On the side that faces you, in its upper third, 1 cm proud of the surface, held by a WebXR anchor. It blinks, follows you, and turns at most 40° toward you.
Poke, pet, flick. A fingertip poke makes it jump. A slow palm stroke makes it purr. A flick sends it spinning and grumpy. In the browser: tap, rub, swipe.
Wake a second. With a key, Muse Spark writes a persona from the label and the neighbours, and ask_object lets them gossip (at most two hops). Without a key you type both lines. Puppet mode, labelled.
The binder

Each woken object is a card. Six spirits at most, because a site gets eight persistent anchors. These two pockets are from a recorded IWER run, not from a real home.
